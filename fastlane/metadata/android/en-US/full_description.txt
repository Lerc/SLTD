Build branching defenses against waves of armored, shielded and regenerating enemies. Choose from eleven maps, specialize your towers, and balance early wave bonuses against the risk of overlapping attacks.

SLTD features touch controls, a tactical archive, pause and double speed, and per-map best scores stored on your device. The game uses Canvas and WebGL for its glowing battlefield.

All game files are included in the app. Play offline without an account, advertisements, tracking, or network permissions.

A current Android System WebView with WebGL support is required. Best scores persist across app updates; a run in progress is kept while the app stays in memory, but is not restored after the app is closed or the operating system kills it.
